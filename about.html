About Us

Faith, family, and the American flag.

Old Glory Flag Service LLC

Old Glory Flag Service LLC provides respectful installation and takedown of American flags throughout Utah County for patriotic holidays. Our customers are homeowners and businesses who want to honor service members, veterans, and national traditions without the hassle of buying poles, climbing ladders, or remembering dates.

We’re a local company — based in Provo, serving every ZIP code from Saratoga Springs to Spanish Fork.

Gavin Rice — Founder

Gavin is a student at Utah Valley University (UVU). He founded Old Glory Flag Service to combine his respect for military service with an honest local business that helps neighbors display pride in their country.

When he’s not studying or running the route, you’ll find him camping in the Wasatch or catching a BYU game.

Service Promise

We arrive on time, treat your property with care, and remove flags after each holiday so they never weather overnight. If a flag ever shows wear, we replace it at no charge.
