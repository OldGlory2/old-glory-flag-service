About Us

Faith, family, and the American flag.

Old Glory Flag Service LLC

Old Glory Flag Service LLC provides respectful installation and takedown of American flags throughout Utah County for patriotic holidays. Our customers are homeowners and businesses who want to honor service members, veterans, and national traditions without the hassle of buying poles, climbing ladders, or remembering dates.

We’re a local company — based in Provo, serving every ZIP code from Saratoga Springs to Spanish Fork.

Gavin Rice — Founder

Gavin is a student at Utah Valley University (UVU). He founded Old Glory Flag Service to combine his respect for military service with an honest local business that helps neighbors display pride in their country.

When he’s not studying or running the route, you’ll find him camping in the Wasatch or catching a BYU game.

Service Promise

We arrive on time, treat your property with care, and remove flags after each holiday so they never weather overnight. If a flag ever shows wear, we replace it at no charge.

Questions? Call 210-799-1368 — someone always answers.

Giving Back to Those Who Served

At Old Glory Flag Service, the flag isn't just a product — it's a symbol of sacrifice. A portion of every subscription is donated to organizations dedicated to supporting veterans. We're proud to contribute to a variety of causes working to ensure no veteran is left behind:

🎖️
Soldier On
Providing housing and support services to homeless veterans nationwide
🦅
DAV — Disabled American Veterans
Empowering veterans to lead high-quality lives with respect and dignity
🏅
Wounded Warrior Project
Connecting, serving, and empowering wounded warriors and their families
🇺🇸
Tunnels to Towers Foundation
Building mortgage-free homes for Gold Star and fallen first responder families
🏠
National Coalition for Homeless Veterans
Ending homelessness among veterans through community-based resource networks

Every flag we raise carries that commitment. Thank you for being part of it.
